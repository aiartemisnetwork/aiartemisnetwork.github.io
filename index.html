COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers. Confident decisions.

What is the difference between your two divisions?

Digital Marketing helps you reach and win customers. Business Management Systems helps you organise and operate the business through custom software such as CRM, inventory, attendance, finance or reporting systems. You can choose either division or combine both.

Can Artemis build a system for my specific business?

Yes. We begin by reviewing your requirements and feasibility, then provide a written scope and quotation. We can explore tailored browser-based systems and Android/iPhone apps depending on the project.

How much does a custom management system cost?

Unlike our marketing packages, custom development does not have a single fixed price. We quote based on modules, number of users, cloud hosting, integrations, deployment and support needs.

Can I pay in instalments?

Project milestones and deposit arrangements can be discussed in your proposal. Card payments will be made available only after secure gateway setup and verification are completed.

Do you provide support after launch?

Support, maintenance, hosting and training are included only as defined in your agreed service or development proposal, so expectations and fees stay clear.
